Auto Scaling Group:
Create Instnce Launch Template (VPC,SUBnet,instance type,os,sg,disk).
Different types of scaling groups:-
1. Simple Scaling Group,operations:-
   a. Add 1 Instance
   b. Remove 1 Instance
   c. Change to 3 Instance
Execution mode: manual,at a cron time, or Reccurence(Repetatiove by days,weeks,months).

2. Target Tracking Scaling Rule: -Like keep CPU target to 70%
3. Predictive scaling group:- Average CP utilization,target 80%.
