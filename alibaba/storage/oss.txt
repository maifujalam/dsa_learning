Infrequent Access, Archive, Cold Archive, or Deep Cold Archive

Storage class	Minimum duration	Duration starts from
Infrequent Access	30 days	Last-Modified time in OSS
Archive	60 days	Last-Modified time in OSS
Cold Archive	180 days	Time the object is transitioned to Cold Archive
Deep Cold Archive	180 days	Time the object is transitioned to Deep Cold Archive

OSS Browser 1.0 support bith partial and resubmable upload by default.
